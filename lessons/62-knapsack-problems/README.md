# Lesson 62: Knapsack Problems: Choose Items Under a Limit

*Part D: Data Structures and Algorithms*

## Summary
- 0/1 knapsack: take an item or leave it.
- The 1D space-optimised version (loop capacity backwards).
- Unbounded knapsack (items reusable).
- Subset sum and partition equal subset sum.

## Complexity
Time O(n · capacity), space O(capacity).

## Coding questions
- Q1 (Easy): Subset sum
- Q2 (Easy): Unbounded knapsack (rod cutting)
- Q3 (Medium): Partition equal subset sum
- Q4 (Hard): Target sum with + and - signs

## Recap
- Choose items under a limit with a 1D table.
- Direction of the capacity loop decides single or repeated use.
- Next: longest common subsequence.
